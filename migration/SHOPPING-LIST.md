## 5. Three judgement calls worth making yourself

**Odyssey shampoo — 15 packs (£283.35) or 10 (£188.90)?**
The 15 comes from the burn-checked rate of 11.42 packs/month, and that rate rests
on a single 8-day window after one delivery. If you'd rather not commit on one data
point, order to the purchase floor instead (6.42/month) and it's 10 packs. Either
way the first two Tuesday counts will settle it, and shampoo is the biggest single
line here.

**Bin bags — is it really 400 a month?**
The minimum of 210 bags rests on an unverified par of 2 packs/month, i.e. ~13 bags
a day. It's only £33 for 2 packs so the downside is small, but if that par is
double the truth you'll see it within a fortnight.

**Printer ink — 1 pack of each colour.**
Flagged because the count of 1 sits exactly on the minimum of 1, which is the
trigger. Reasonable to hold one spare set, but check the £224.90 price on the old
record first — that looks like a full multipack booked against a single cartridge.
