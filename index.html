Counts, minimums and usage are all in items — rolls, bottles, cans — never packs. Deliveries are logged in packs and converted with the product's pack size. Est. stock = last count + deliveries since − usage since (usage = measured from consecutive counts where available, otherwise the figure on record). "Cover" is days of stock left. An item flags Order now when it drops below its minimum, or under 2 days' cover, or — with no count yet — when ~a month has passed since the last order. Suggested order tops up to the minimum plus a month of usage, or plus one pack where no usage figure exists, and is shown in packs because that is what you buy. Where a pack size or minimum is missing the order is left blank with the reason, rather than computed through the gap.

Counts are entered in the Consumables Stock Take Google Sheet and appear here automatically. This view is read-only.
